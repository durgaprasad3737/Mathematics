# Math Notes for AI & ML

A collection of handwritten mathematics notes for anyone who wants to study **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning**. They are especially useful if you want to understand the basics, such as **matrices and logic**, before moving into the algorithms.

## Who these notes are for

- Mathematics students who want to move into AI and ML
- Beginners who want strong foundations before touching the algorithms
- Anyone who learns better from worked, step-by-step examples than from dense theory

## Why this maths matters for AI and ML

Machine learning is built on a small set of mathematical ideas:

- **Matrices and vectors** represent data and transformations
- **Calculus** (limits, differentiation, integration) is how models learn through gradients and optimisation
- **Probability and statistics** underlie prediction, uncertainty and evaluation
- **Logic and reasoning** support decision-making and proofs
- **Functions, sequences and logarithms** appear in loss functions, scaling and information theory

Understanding these basics first makes everything that comes later much easier to follow.

## How to use these notes

1. Read the concept or definition at the start of each section.
2. Cover the solution and try each worked example yourself.
3. Check your steps against the notes, including the mistakes that are marked.
